Novelty and Positioning Pack
============================

Project Position
----------------
This work should be positioned as an implementation-backed simulation study
of secure trust-based V2X communication with blockchain-assisted trust
continuity and privacy-aware operation.

One-Line Positioning
--------------------
An implementation-backed ns-3 study of secure trust-based V2X communication
that combines packet validation, trust-guided RSU handover, blockchain-assisted
trust continuity, and privacy-aware operation under simulated attack conditions.

Three-Line Positioning
----------------------
The project does not present a generic blockchain-heavy V2X proposal.
Instead, it studies how replay-aware packet validation, trust-guided
FAST/FULL/REJECT handover, and compact blockchain-assisted trust continuity
can work together in a mobility-aware simulated environment.

Why This Is Strong
------------------
The strength of the work is not that every component is individually new.
The strength is that the project integrates:
- packet-level security filtering
- trust-guided handover control
- continuity support across RSUs
- cache-aware blockchain-assisted lookup
- optional privacy-aware operation
- formal analysis of the handover authentication subprotocol

Safe Novelty Statement
----------------------
The novelty lies in the integrated evaluation of security filtering,
trust-guided handover, blockchain-assisted trust continuity, and privacy-aware
operation in a single implementation-backed ns-3 framework, together with a
bounded formal analysis of the handover authentication subprotocol.

Reviewer-Friendly One-Paragraph Version
---------------------------------------
Unlike studies that isolate only authentication, only trust scoring, or only
blockchain logging, this project evaluates how these mechanisms interact in a
mobility-aware V2X setting. The framework uses message-level validation for
replay and invalid-packet filtering, trust-guided FAST/FULL/REJECT handover
decisions during RSU transition, and a compact blockchain-assisted continuity
layer with cache support for trust-state retrieval. This gives the work a
clear implementation-and-evaluation focus rather than a purely conceptual one.

Viva-Style Answer
-----------------
What is new is not a claim of inventing blockchain or trust for V2X in
isolation. The contribution is that the project operationalizes them together:
security filtering at the packet path, trust-based control at handover, and
continuity support across RSUs using a compact blockchain-assisted lookup
mechanism, then evaluates the trade-offs under simulated attacks and mobility.

Do Not Claim
------------
- full end-to-end formal proof of the whole framework
- production-ready blockchain platform
- CK or eCK proof
- full Dolev-Yao proof of the complete architecture
- formal anonymity proof
