Formal Analysis Boundary
========================

Tool Used
---------
Scyther

What Is Formally Analyzed
-------------------------
Only the handover authentication subprotocol is formally modeled and checked.

Model Evolution
---------------
1. Initial baseline authentication model
   - insecure
   - multiple secrecy/agreement claims fail

2. Intermediate secure model
   - stronger identity binding and signed/encrypted payloads
   - most claims pass, but initiator-side agreement/synchronization remains incomplete

3. Final authenticated model
   - adds responder freshness and explicit final acknowledgement
   - target claims pass within Scyther analysis bounds

Claims Supported by Formal Analysis
-----------------------------------
- secrecy of modeled handover token/material inside the final protocol model
- aliveness
- weak agreement
- non-injective agreement
- synchronization

What Is NOT Formally Claimed
----------------------------
- full proof of the complete V2X framework
- proof of the blockchain subsystem
- proof of the full trust engine
- proof of privacy / anonymity properties
- CK / eCK proof
- full Dolev-Yao proof over the whole architecture

Safe Paper Wording
------------------
The paper may state that the handover authentication subprotocol was modeled
in Scyther and that the final protocol variant satisfied the intended secrecy,
aliveness, agreement, and synchronization claims within the analysis bounds.

Unsafe Wording
--------------
Do not state:
- the whole framework is formally proven secure
- the full system satisfies CK / eCK security
- the complete architecture is proven under Dolev-Yao
- privacy is formally proven
