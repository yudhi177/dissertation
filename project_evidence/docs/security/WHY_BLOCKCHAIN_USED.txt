Why Blockchain Is Used
======================

Purpose
-------
This document explains why the project uses a blockchain-assisted component
and how that use should be presented safely in the dissertation and paper.

Core Reason
-----------
The blockchain-assisted component is used to support trust continuity across
RSUs during mobility.

In this project, the blockchain is not introduced as a generic trend or
hype element. Its role is specific and functional.

Problem Without Blockchain Support
----------------------------------
In a purely local trust design, trust information may remain tied to the
current observing RSU.

This creates a continuity problem when a vehicle moves from one RSU region
to another:

- the next RSU may not have prior trust context
- trust may need to be rebuilt from limited local observation
- handover decisions may become slower or less informed
- stale or missing trust history may reduce decision quality

Why Blockchain-Assisted Support Helps
-------------------------------------
The blockchain-assisted path is used to make compact trust-related state
available beyond a single local RSU.

This supports:

- trust continuity across infrastructure boundaries
- tamper-evident recording of trust-related updates
- better availability of prior trust context at handover time
- less dependence on restarting trust from zero at each RSU boundary

Operational Role
----------------
The blockchain-assisted layer does not replace packet validation,
trust logic, or handover policy.

Instead, it supports them by providing continuity-oriented trust lookup.

The operational sequence is:

- trust-related state is recorded in compact form
- another RSU can later query or retrieve that state
- cache support reduces repeated lookup cost
- the target RSU still applies local freshness checks and thresholds
- final handover decision remains FAST, FULL, or REJECT

What This Project Does NOT Claim
--------------------------------
This project does not claim:

- a full production blockchain deployment
- a consensus-heavy real-world ledger implementation
- full raw traffic storage on-chain
- a complete decentralized economic model
- blockchain as the only security mechanism

So the blockchain should be described as a support layer for trust
continuity, not as the main source of security by itself.

Why It Matters in This Framework
--------------------------------
The project combines:

- packet-level filtering
- trust-guided handover control
- mobility across RSU regions
- optional privacy support

In such a setting, continuity of trust information becomes important.

The blockchain-assisted design helps preserve useful trust context during
mobility while allowing cache-enabled evaluation of overhead.

Reviewer-Friendly Positioning
-----------------------------
The blockchain component is included to address a specific continuity
problem in RSU-based trust management: how to preserve and retrieve compact
trust-related state when vehicles move between infrastructure regions.

Safe Paper Wording
------------------
The blockchain-assisted layer is used to support trust continuity across
RSUs by recording and retrieving compact trust-related state. In this
framework, blockchain is a continuity-support mechanism rather than a
generic or production-scale ledger claim.
